# Surface brief: Systo website (site/)

Scope: public marketing site at systo.mohammedibrahim.dev: home (/) and changelog (/changelog). Mode: Persuade (changelog page is Read).
Audience: Mac and Windows users who find Activity Monitor / Task Manager opaque, plus developers who saw the launch post. Job: understand what Systo is in one viewport, believe it is real and light, download the right installer. Proof: the actual Dynamic Island and Processes UI rendered live, real versions and release notes from CHANGELOG.md and GitHub Releases. Constraints: static deploy, no tracking, downloads link to GitHub Releases assets, installers are unsigned (say so).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page opens on the Dynamic Island itself, working, not a screenshot of it; it refuses the split hero (headline left, product shot right, logo row under).
OWN-WORLD: Systo's own monochrome: OKLCH neutrals, Geist and Geist Mono with ss01/cv11, 0.625rem radii, hairline borders, the black island pill, heat-shaded table cells, tabular numerals. No accent colour.
STORY: The visitor hovers a pill at the top of a laptop bezel, it springs open into rings and top apps; they flip macOS/Windows and watch it become the floating pill; they read "Activity Monitor, minus the squinting", scroll the live Processes replica and three short proofs, then download the installer for their OS.
